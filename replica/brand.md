# Brand: PDFKraft

Following replica-brand: a name, a palette, a mark and a voice of its own, then a sweep of the
tree for anything of the original that is not attribution.

## Name

**PDFKraft** (given by the project owner). Checks to run before selling anything under it: a
trademark search in the markets you sell in (EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database), the
`pdfkraft` handles on the app stores you use, and the domain you want. None of that was done
here.

Identifiers derived from it: crates `pdfkraft-*`, binaries `pdfkraft` and `pdfkraft-cli`, app id
`io.github.kompheak7711.pdfkraft` (the Flathub convention for GitHub-hosted projects),
environment variables `PDFKRAFT_*`, settings and cache directories named `pdfkraft`.

## Palette and mark

| role | hex | used for |
|---|---|---|
| PDFKraft blue | `#1b3a6b` | the icon's field, the DMG background panel |
| Paper | `#f4f1ea` | the K |
| Khmer red | `#c8102e` | the dot (the red of the Cambodian flag, next to its blue) |

The mark is a K drawn as three butt-capped strokes with a dot, on a rounded tile
(`assets/app-icon/pdfkraft.svg`). It is geometric on purpose: no font, nothing traced, and
regenerated by `packaging/icons.py` from the standard library alone. The interface theme tokens
are unchanged from upstream (they are the app's own design, not a trademark).

## Voice

Plain sentences, the feature first, limits stated next to the feature. The README, the About
dialog and the home card say what the app does (types Khmer), what it is built on (PdfCraft) and
where to go for help (the repository's issues). No community to join, no Discord.

## Sweep

Run: `python3 replica-brand/sweep.py . --avoid "ArtCraft,Storyteller,getartcraft" --domains
"getartcraft.com,discord.gg" --colors "#12a58a"` from the replica-skill checkout.

Before the rebrand it reported several hundred hits (every translation catalog carried the old
community strings). After it, what remains is deliberate:

| hit | why it stays |
|---|---|
| `NOTICE`, `LICENSE-MIT`, `README.md`, `AGENTS.md` naming ArtCraft Team / PdfCraft | copyright attribution the MIT and Apache licences require, and the statement that the trademarks were removed |
| `crates/ui-egui/src/dialogs.rs`, `links.rs`, `commands.rs`: "Based on PdfCraft" | the credit link in the About dialog and Help menu |
| `crates/ui-egui/tests/it/links.rs` asserting no "ArtCraft" label | the test that keeps the trademark out |
| `xtask/src/assets.rs` `LicenseRef-Storyteller-Trademark` | the asset policy still refuses such an entry anywhere but a directory that no longer exists |
| `ROADMAP.md` | upstream's history, kept as history |
| `assets/app-icon/pdfkraft-document.svg` badge colour `#12a58a` | the Windows document-type icon; its `.ico` is a render of this SVG and there is no SVG rasteriser in this environment to re-render it, so the badge keeps upstream's green for now |
| `packaging/macos/dmg/background.tiff` | the same: a render that needs a Mac to regenerate (`packaging/macos/dmg/README.md`) |

Everything else of the original's name, domains and community links is gone: the title bar
button, the home card, the About dialog, the Help menu, the CLI banner, the packaging metadata
(AppStream, nfpm, WiX, macOS credits), the screenshots and the brand directory.
